## Cleanroom Injection Molding at Production Scale

Cleanroom injection molding is not a different machine; it is a different discipline. The part is the same plastic, but every touch, every airflow, and every package is controlled to protect it.

Clean programs protect the part after it leaves the mold. Handling, inspection, and packaging happen under controlled conditions, and we document the chain so a medical or optical buyer receives a part that meets its spec.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which cleanroom injection molding stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 400T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.02 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 300,000 - 1,000,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 25 - 40 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 20,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Processes run under ISO 13485-aligned controls with clean handling and packaging. We match the control level to the part risk profile and document it per lot.
