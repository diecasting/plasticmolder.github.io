## Polypropylene (PP) Injection Molding at Production Scale

Polypropylene injection molding is the low-cost, high-volume workhorse for caps, containers, and anything with a living hinge. PP resists chemicals, shrugs off fatigue, and molds fast, which keeps part cost down.

The one thing PP demands is shrinkage awareness. Its molded shrinkage is higher than most resins, so the tool must be cut to suit. Get that wrong and every part is undersized; get it right and PP runs almost forever.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which polypropylene (pp) injection molding stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 800T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.03 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 500,000 - 1,000,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 20 - 35 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 60,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Material grade is selected for hinge life, clarity, or chemical duty. We size tools for PP shrinkage and validate living-hinge cycle life on tryout before release.
