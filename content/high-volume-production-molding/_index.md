## High-Volume Production Molding at Production Scale

High-volume production molding is where process discipline becomes economics. The part may be simple, but making a million of them identically, at cost, is the hard part.

Volume programs win or lose on variation. We run multi-cavity and stack tools with automated cells and SPC, so the thousandth part equals the first and scrap stays predictable and low.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which high-volume production molding stays predictable. A press range of 80T - 800T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.03 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 1,000,000+ shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 25 - 45 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 200,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Tooling is specified for 1,000,000+ shot life with planned maintenance. We run SPC and automated inspection to hold critical dimensions across the full run.
