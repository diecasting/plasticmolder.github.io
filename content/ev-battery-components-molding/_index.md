## EV & Battery Plastic Components Molding at Production Scale

EV and battery plastic components carry a safety burden that commodity parts do not. Connectors, housings, and busbar holders must resist heat, flame, and thermal cycling while keeping dimensional and sealing integrity.

These programs are specified, not guessed. Resin grade, flame class, and traceability are written into the print, and we validate them on first article and hold them across the run with closed-loop process control.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which ev & battery plastic components molding stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 800T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.02 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 300,000 - 1,000,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 20 - 40 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 40,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Material grades are selected for flame class and thermal duty and documented per lot. Insert-molded busbar and terminal position is validated before production release.
