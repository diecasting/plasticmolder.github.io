## Plastic Gears & Bushings Molding at Production Scale

Plastic gears and bushings move from the catalog to the product only when tooth geometry and wear are controlled. A gear that binds or a bushing that wears early fails the whole motion system.

Gear programs are about repeatable geometry. Cavity steel, gate location, and pack pressure set the tooth form; we verify it with gauges and CMM and hold it across the run so every gear meshes the same.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which plastic gears & bushings molding stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 400T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.02 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 500,000 - 1,000,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 20 - 35 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 50,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Material is matched to load, speed, and lubrication; POM leads for low friction, PEEK for heat and chemical duty. We validate tooth geometry and wear on tryout.
