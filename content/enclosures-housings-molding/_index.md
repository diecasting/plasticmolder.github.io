## Enclosures & Housings Molding at Production Scale

Enclosures and housings are the most-seen part a molding supplier makes, and the most judged. Fit, finish, and feel decide whether a product looks premium or cheap on the shelf.

An enclosure program balances wall thickness, cosmetic surface, and assembly fit. We design gates and vents around the visible face and lock the process so every part matches the approved master sample.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which enclosures & housings molding stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 800T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.03 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 300,000 - 1,000,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 20 - 40 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 50,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Material grade is matched to finish, heat, and impact needs; EMI options are available for electronics. We control cosmetic surfaces against an approved master sample.
