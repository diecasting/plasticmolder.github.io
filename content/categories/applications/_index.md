## Application Determines the Process

A plastic part is never molded in isolation — it is molded for a job. An enclosure must look right and seal; a connector must hold tolerance and insulate; a battery component must not fail under thermal load. The application decides which resin, which press tonnage, and which inspection regime actually matter.

That is why our application pages describe failure modes, not just features. Understanding where a part fails is what lets process controls prevent it.

## Application Verticals

- **Enclosures & Housings** — cosmetic and sealed shells for electronics and industrial equipment. See [enclosures and housings molding](/en/enclosures-housings-molding/).
- **EV & Battery Components** — flame-retardant, traceable parts for charging and storage. See [EV and battery plastic components](/en/ev-battery-components-molding/).
- **Medical Device Components** — ISO 13485-controlled, cleanroom-compatible molding. See [medical device components molding](/en/medical-device-components-molding/).
- **Connectors & Terminals** — dimensional precision and electrical insulation. See [plastic connectors and terminals](/en/plastic-connectors-molding/).
- **Gears & Bushings** — wear resistance and geometric accuracy. See [plastic gears and bushings molding](/en/plastic-gears-bushings-molding/).

## Building the Right Program

Each application is scoped from the same disciplined path: design for manufacturability, tool validation, resin qualification, controlled molding, and verified shipment. For regulated fields, the records your audit team needs are generated as the parts are made, not reconstructed afterward.
