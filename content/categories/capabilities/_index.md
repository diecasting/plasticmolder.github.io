## Capability Is How Promises Become Repeatable

Specifications on a website are commitments about what can actually be delivered. A 50-ton to 800-ton press range, closed-loop control, and in-house tooling are not decorations — together they are what let a program hold tolerance across every shipment for the life of the contract.

The capability pages below describe how each process is run and where it creates value.

## Capability Areas

- **Assembly & Secondary Operations** — welding, printing, and assembly that can lower landed cost versus multiple suppliers. See [assembly and secondary operations](/en/assembly-secondary-operations/).
- **Cleanroom Molding** — controlled-environment production for medical and high-purity parts. See [cleanroom injection molding](/en/cleanroom-injection-molding/).
- **High-Volume Production** — multi-cavity runs with SPC and cavity-pressure monitoring. See [high-volume production molding](/en/high-volume-production-molding/).
- **Rapid Tooling** — fast bridge and prototype tooling to validate form, fit, and function. See [rapid tooling](/en/rapid-tooling/).
- **Thin-Wall Molding** — cycle-time and material savings for packaging and electronics. See [thin-wall injection molding](/en/thin-wall-injection-molding/).

Related precision capabilities include [precision injection molding](/en/precision-injection-molding/), [plastic injection mold tooling](/en/plastic-injection-mold-tooling/), and [precision plastic components](/en/precision-plastic-components/).
