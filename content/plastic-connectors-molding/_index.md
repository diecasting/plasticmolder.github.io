## Plastic Connectors & Terminals Molding at Production Scale

Plastic connectors and terminals are where molding precision meets electrical reliability. A connector that does not mate on the line, or drifts out of tolerance after reflow, becomes a recall risk rather than a component.

Connector programs live or die on consistency. Resin lot, mold temperature, and cavity pressure must stay inside a narrow band so every part mates the same way. We run closed-loop control and verify critical features on every setup.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which plastic connectors & terminals molding stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 400T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.01 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 500,000 - 1,000,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 20 - 35 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 80,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Material grade is matched to reflow temperature and flammability needs; LCP is used where signal integrity and warp control matter. We validate insert-molded terminal position and seal integrity before release.
