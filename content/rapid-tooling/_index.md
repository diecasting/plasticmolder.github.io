## Rapid Tooling & Fast Tooling at Production Scale

Rapid tooling is the shortcut that is not a compromise. It builds a real, moldable tool in days so you can validate the part, the market, and the process before betting on hardened steel.

The trade is tool life, not part quality. Aluminum and pre-hardened steel tools make genuine production parts for bridge and pilot runs; we tell you exactly where the volume ceiling sits before you commit.

## What the Capability Numbers Mean for Your Program

The specifications define the envelope in which rapid tooling & fast tooling stays predictable. A press range of 50T - 400T covers parts from small precision features to larger functional components without splitting the program across vendors. Tolerances of ±0.05 mm describe where critical features are controlled rather than left to chance.

Mold life rated at 10,000 - 100,000 shots is a maintenance plan, not a sales figure. Tools are serviced by shot count so wear never surprises a shipment. Tooling lead time of 7 - 20 days reflects complexity and validation, and an annual capacity above 1,000,000 parts signals a floor built for sustained volume.

## How a Program Runs

1. **Design for manufacturability.** Geometry, gate location, and draft are reviewed so the part molds predictably and meets function.
2. **Material and tool selection.** Resin grade and tool steel are chosen for the performance, volume, and life the program requires.
3. **Tool fabrication.** The mold is machined, with hot-runner or multi-cavity features where the program specifies them.
4. **Molding and process setup.** Parameters are developed and locked so fill, pack, and cooling stay inside the validated window.
5. **In-process verification.** Critical dimensions are checked with gauges and CMM; results are recorded for traceability.
6. **Packaging and shipment.** Parts are inspected, packed, and shipped against the agreed quality and delivery plan.

## Material and Application Fit

Tool material is matched to volume and life; aluminum suits bridge runs, pre-hardened steel extends life. We quote lead time and shot life up front so the decision is clear.
